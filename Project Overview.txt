ABSTRACT 
In the field of agriculture, efficient use of resources such as fertilizers is crucial for 
sustainable farming. Overuse of chemicals can lead to environmental damage and health risks 
to farmers, while underuse may lead to poor crop yields. A potential solution to this problem 
is the implementation of an autonomous, targeted spraying robot. This project presents a 
novel robotic system that leverages artificial intelligence (AI) and image processing 
techniques to detect targeted plants in real time and autonomously spray fertilizers. The robot 
is equipped with a camera for image capture, which transmits data to a laptop for processing. 
The AI algorithms on the laptop analyse the captured images to identify the plants. The robot 
is manually controlled using a Bluetooth app for movement, while the spraying of the liquid 
is automatically triggered when the target plant is identified. The robot, constructed using DC 
motors, a motor driver, and a microcontroller, operates with high precision, reducing 
chemical waste, protecting farmers from hazardous exposure, and enhancing crop 
productivity. This project demonstrates how targeted applications of chemicals can optimize 
agricultural practices, contributing to environmental conservation and improving farmer 
safety.

INTRODUCTION 
Agriculture is the backbone of many economies worldwide, and the use of fertilizers plays 
a significant role in maintaining crop health and ensuring high yields. However, the 
indiscriminate use of chemicals in farming has led to several problems, including 
environmental pollution, soil degradation, and health hazards to farmers who are exposed 
to these toxic substances. One of the major challenges in modern agriculture is to find ways 
to optimize the use of these chemicals, applying them only where necessary and in 
appropriate amounts. In response to these challenges, advancements in robotics and 
artificial intelligence (AI) offer promising solutions. Autonomous robots, equipped with 
AI-powered systems, have the potential to transform traditional farming practices by 
providing more precise and targeted interventions. One such application is the development 
of robots for fertilizer spraying. These robots can significantly reduce the amount of 
chemicals used by applying them only to the plants that need them. 
By utilizing AI and image processing techniques, the robot can identify target plants in real
time, allowing for highly accurate spraying. The system operates with minimal human 
intervention, as the robot’s movement is controlled manually through a Bluetooth app, while 
the spraying function is automated. This project combines the benefits of automation, 
precision agriculture, and safety to create an effective tool for modern farming. 
1.1
Overview 
The targeted fertilizer spraying robot presented in this project is designed to address the 
challenges of traditional spraying methods by providing a more precise, efficient, and safe 
solution. The robot is equipped with a camera that captures images of plants as it moves 
through the field. 
These images are processed on a laptop using AI algorithms to identify the target plants. 
Once a target plant is detected, the robot autonomously sprays the appropriate fertilizer. 
The robot’s movement is controlled manually via a Bluetooth app, allowing the user to 
guide it through the field. The robot is constructed using four DC motors, a motor driver, 
and an Arduino microcontroller.

1.4
Problem Statement 
Traditional fertilizer application methods involve spraying large areas of farmland, which 
results in the excessive use of chemicals. This blanket approach not only wastes resources 
but also poses health risks to farmers and leads to environmental contamination. Over time, 
this can degrade soil health, contaminate water sources, and harm non-target organisms, 
including beneficial insects. Additionally, farmers who work with these chemicals are at 
constant risk of inhaling hazardous substances, which can lead to severe health issues. 
The indiscriminate spraying methods also fail to take into account the varying needs of 
individual plants. Some plants may require more or less fertilizer depending on their health 
and growth stage, and blanket spraying does not provide the specificity required for 
optimal crop management. Thus, there is a need for a more efficient, targeted approach to 
chemical application that conserves resources, protects the environment.

Proposed System 
The proposed system is an integrated agriculture robot designed to automate key farming 
tasks such as fertilization using an Arduino Uno microcontroller. The robot will be 
equipped with a moisture sensor, relay modules for controlling devices, and Bluetooth 
technology to allow remote control and monitoring via a smartphone application. The 
system aims to improve farming efficiency, reduce manual labour, and ensure optimal crop 
care, making it more accessible and cost-effective for small to medium-scale farmers.
